The Bottleneck

Autoimmune care is administratively heavy — and reimbursed like it isn't.

A typical autoimmune patient doesn't move through your system in a straight line. They move through this:

Specialist appointment
→
Labs
→
Another appointment
→
Portal triage
→
Another specialist
→
Biologic PA
→
Follow-up

Every step in that chain carries real administrative cost — prior auths, portal triage, care coordination — that your E&M reimbursement was never priced to cover.

Net contribution  =  reimbursement  −  fully loaded cost of delivering the visit
Autoimmune care is exactly where this math gets uncomfortable: the administrative intensity is disproportionate to the E&M reimbursement attached to it. Meanwhile, your appointment backlog keeps growing — and the complex new patients who actually need your specialists are the ones waiting months to get in.
The Thymos Solution

Why health systems partner with Thymos

Thymos quarterbacks the daily work of autoimmune management and continuous coordination with your specialists — lowering total cost of care while optimizing your capacity.

Easy to Implement

Start focused. Prove the impact. Scale.

Thymos extends your existing specialty infrastructure without requiring additional specialist headcount or an enterprise-wide transformation.

1
Identify the opportunity
Start with the specialty, patient population, or access bottleneck where additional capacity can create the most value. Initial analysis can begin with de-identified data.
2
Fit into your existing workflows
Together, we define referral criteria, clinical responsibilities, communication workflows, and clear escalation pathways back to your specialists.
3
Launch a focused pilot
Begin with one service line, geography, or patient cohort. Thymos provides the physician-led longitudinal care infrastructure without requiring your system to build a new program from scratch.
4
Measure, then scale
Track the outcomes that matter to your system, including specialist capacity, access, utilization, cost, patient experience, and appropriate escalation. Expand across specialties as the model demonstrates value.
No enterprise-wide transformation on day one. Start with one population, prove the value, and scale from there.

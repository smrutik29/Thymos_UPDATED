Washington Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective May 16, 2025  ·  Last Updated September 9, 2026

This policy addresses "consumer health data" of Washington residents that is subject to Washington's My Health My Data Act. Much of the health information Thymos handles is Protected Health Information under HIPAA or is otherwise exempt from that Act; where information is subject to the Act, this policy applies.

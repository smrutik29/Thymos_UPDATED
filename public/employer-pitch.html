What Thymos Does

Clinical support
for the taper path.

Thymos pairs autoimmune patients with clinicians who actively work toward reducing — and eventually eliminating — their biologic medication. Safely. Measurably.

🩺
Clinician-led program
Patients work with Thymos clinicians to stabilize symptoms and identify taper windows. Not just monitoring — active intervention.
📉
Measured by actual fills skipped
We track real pharmacy data: prescriptions available but not filled. That's the clinical signal and the cost signal — same metric.
🔒
De-identified to you
Employers receive monthly reports with aggregate and per-patient-code data. No names, no diagnoses. Just proof of savings.

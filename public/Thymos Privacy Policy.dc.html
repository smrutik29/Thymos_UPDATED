Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2025

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Thymos Health, Inc. and Thymos Provider Group, PC collect, use, and protect personal and health information.

2. Information Collected

Information collected may include personal information you voluntarily provide, including: account details, health information submitted for care, device data, usage data, and payment information.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to provide telehealth services, coordinate care, operate the platform, ensure security, and comply with legal obligations.

4. HIPAA

Medical information is protected under HIPAA through the Provider Group's Notice of Privacy Practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal or health information. Information may be shared with clinicians, vendors, or regulators as required by law.

6. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data subject to applicable law.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained as required by law and clinical standards.

9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without guardian consent.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, reach out to privacy@thymoshealth.com

Patient journeys

Common diagnoses and typical disease journeys

Autoimmune and inflammatory conditions are often addressed inefficiently. Each patient journey below walks through how a specific condition typically gets diagnosed and treated (drawn from published clinical research) — so you know what to expect.

Alopecia areata → Ankylosing spondylitis → Autoimmune hepatitis → Crohn's disease → Dermatomyositis → Guillain-Barré syndrome → Hashimoto's thyroiditis → Multiple sclerosis → Myasthenia gravis → Polymyositis → Psoriasis → Psoriatic arthritis → Rheumatoid arthritis → Sjögren's syndrome → Systemic lupus erythematosus → Ulcerative colitis →

Every patient's course is different — these journeys describe common patterns, not a guaranteed path. Don't see your condition? Browse all conditions we treat →

Individual results vary. Thymos Health resources are for general education and are not a substitute for evaluation by a qualified clinician. Medication changes should be directed by the appropriate treating clinician based on individual disease activity and clinical judgment.

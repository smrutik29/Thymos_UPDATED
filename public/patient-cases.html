Complex autoimmune presentations Thymos co-manages

Case 01 — Myalgic Encephalomyelitis / Chronic Fatigue Syndrome

A 57-year-old woman had progressively worsening fatigue, recurrent hives, brain fog, polyarthralgia and IBS-like symptoms over several years. Primary care, gastroenterology, rheumatology, orthopedics and neurology had not produced lasting improvement. Primary diagnoses ultimately included:

Chronic fatigue syndrome (ME/CFS) Iron deficiency anemia Dyslipidemia / cardiovascular risk Vitamin D deficiency Subclinical hypothyroidism Disorder of protein metabolism

Case 02 — Undifferentiated Connective Tissue Disease

A 47-year-old woman had persistent multiple joint pain, cold extremities and rashes while taking methotrexate and hydroxychloroquine. She also reported recurrent mouth ulcers, dry eyes, episodic low-grade fevers and profound fatigue after seeing primary care, two dermatologists and a rheumatologist. Primary diagnoses ultimately included:

Undifferentiated connective tissue disease (UCTD) Nutritional anemia Vitamin D deficiency Elevated hemoglobin A1c Hyperhomocysteinemia Hyperferritinemia

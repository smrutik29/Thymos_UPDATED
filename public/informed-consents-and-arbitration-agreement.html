Thymos Health

Informed Consents and Arbitration Agreement

Effective Date: May 16, 2025

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Additional patient agreements are presented here for review and acceptance in the Thymos Health mobile application where applicable.

The attached agreements are:

  • Addendum A: Consent to Treatment
  • Addendum B: Telehealth Consent
  • Addendum C: Audio Recording and AI-Assisted Documentation Consent
  • Addendum D: California Medical Services Arbitration Agreement

Addendum A — Consent to Treatment

Separate patient acceptance required.

This Consent to Treatment applies to healthcare provided through Thymos Provider Group, PC.

1. Consent to Healthcare

I voluntarily consent to evaluation, diagnosis, treatment, care coordination, monitoring, and other healthcare services provided by authorized clinicians through Thymos Provider Group.

I understand that the exact nature of my care will depend on my medical condition, clinical findings, applicable standards of care, clinician judgment, and applicable law.

2. Clinical Decision-Making

I understand that my treating clinician will make clinical decisions based on information including my symptoms; history; diagnoses; medical records; laboratory and diagnostic results; medications; treatment response; disease activity; risks and contraindications; and other relevant clinical information.

I understand that no treatment, medication, laboratory test, referral, or particular outcome is guaranteed.

3. My Responsibilities

I agree to provide medical information truthfully and to the best of my ability. I understand that incomplete or inaccurate information can affect clinical assessment and treatment.

I will inform my care team of significant changes in my symptoms, medications, allergies, health status, or other information relevant to my care.

4. Risks, Benefits, and Alternatives

Healthcare may involve risks, benefits, side effects, uncertainties, and alternatives. My clinician may discuss these matters with me as clinically appropriate.

I have the right to ask questions and participate in decisions about my care.

5. Right to Refuse

I may refuse a proposed treatment, medication, diagnostic test, or other healthcare intervention.

I understand that refusing recommended care may affect the ability of Thymos clinicians to diagnose or manage my condition safely.

6. Prescriptions

I understand that prescribing is based solely on the professional judgment of an authorized clinician and applicable law.

Enrollment in Thymos does not guarantee that any medication will be prescribed, continued, changed, tapered, or discontinued.

7. Coordination With Other Providers

At my request or as otherwise permitted by law, Thymos may communicate or coordinate with other healthcare professionals involved in my care.

I understand that Thymos may recommend that I seek in-person evaluation, emergency care, specialty consultation, or care outside Thymos where clinically appropriate.

8. Emergency Care

I understand that Thymos is not an emergency service. If I experience an emergency, I will call 911 or seek immediate emergency care.

9. No Guarantee of Outcome

Medicine is not an exact science.

I understand that Thymos cannot guarantee diagnosis, symptom improvement, remission, treatment response, medication reduction, or any particular medical outcome.

10. Privacy

My health information will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the Thymos Provider Group Notice of Privacy Practices.

11. Consent

By separately accepting this Consent to Treatment in the Thymos application, I confirm that I have read and understood it, have had an opportunity to ask questions, and voluntarily consent to treatment through Thymos Provider Group.

Addendum B — Telehealth Consent

Separate patient acceptance required.

This Telehealth Consent applies to healthcare provided through Thymos Provider Group, PC using telehealth.

1. Nature of Telehealth

Telehealth involves providing healthcare when the patient and healthcare professional are in different locations through electronic communications technology. Telehealth may include:

  • •real-time video and audio visits;
  • •secure electronic messaging;
  • •transmission and review of health records;
  • •remote review of laboratory and diagnostic information; and
  • •other electronic communication supporting care.

2. Consent to Telehealth

I voluntarily consent to receive healthcare through telehealth.

I understand that I may ask questions about telehealth and that, where clinically appropriate and reasonably available, I may discuss alternative methods of receiving care.

3. Potential Benefits

Potential benefits may include:

  • •improved access to specialty care;
  • •reduced travel;
  • •faster communication;
  • •greater continuity between scheduled visits; and
  • •easier sharing and review of clinical information.

4. Potential Risks and Limitations

I understand that telehealth can involve limitations or risks, including:

  • •inability to perform a complete hands-on physical examination;
  • •limitations in visual or audio quality;
  • •technology or connectivity failure;
  • •delayed or incomplete transmission of information;
  • •potential need for in-person examination or testing;
  • •privacy or security risks inherent in electronic communication; and
  • •clinical circumstances in which telehealth is not appropriate.

My clinician may determine that an in-person evaluation or another form of care is necessary.

5. Patient Location

I understand that healthcare licensing requirements generally depend on where I am physically located when care is provided. I agree to accurately provide my physical location when requested.

Thymos may postpone, limit, or discontinue a telehealth encounter if the applicable clinician is not legally authorized to provide care at my location.

6. Traveling Outside the United States

If I am an established Thymos patient temporarily traveling outside the United States, I understand that Thymos may be unable to provide some or all clinical services depending on applicable law, professional requirements, safety considerations, and the circumstances of my care.

7. Privacy

Telehealth encounters are subject to applicable healthcare confidentiality and privacy laws.

I understand that I should participate in telehealth from a location that provides an appropriate level of privacy when reasonably possible.

8. Technology Failure

If a telehealth connection fails, Thymos may attempt to reconnect, use another legally appropriate communication method, reschedule the encounter, or advise me to obtain care elsewhere.

9. Emergencies

Telehealth through Thymos is not an emergency service. If an emergency occurs during a visit, Thymos clinicians may take steps permitted by law to facilitate emergency assistance.

10. Recording

My consent to telehealth does not automatically authorize Thymos to record an encounter. Audio recording is governed by a separate Audio Recording and AI-Assisted Documentation Consent.

11. Withdrawal

I may withdraw my consent to future telehealth care, subject to the consequences of no longer being able to receive Services that can only reasonably be provided through telehealth.

Withdrawal does not invalidate care already provided.

12. Consent

By separately accepting this Telehealth Consent in the Thymos application, I acknowledge that I have been informed about the use of telehealth and voluntarily consent to telehealth as a mode of receiving healthcare.

Addendum C — Audio Recording and AI-Assisted Documentation Consent

Separate patient acceptance required.

This Consent applies to optional audio recording and AI-assisted documentation used during certain Thymos telehealth visits.

1. What Is Recorded

With my affirmative consent, audio from a telehealth visit may be recorded. Video is not recorded. I understand that Thymos will provide notice before recording begins.

2. Consent at Each Visit

I understand that accepting this Consent during onboarding does not eliminate the additional notice and consent provided before an individual recorded visit. Thymos will ask for my affirmative consent before recording begins.

3. Recording Is Optional

I may decline audio recording and still receive the underlying telehealth visit. Declining recording will not, by itself, cause Thymos to refuse the visit.

4. Purpose

Audio may be securely processed to:

  • •create a temporary transcript;
  • •create an AI-assisted draft summary;
  • •assist my clinician in preparing the clinical note;
  • •evaluate documentation quality;
  • •conduct quality assurance;
  • •evaluate clinician performance;
  • •perform audits; and
  • •conduct internal validation and quality improvement.

5. AI-Assisted Documentation

I understand that artificial intelligence may assist in transcribing or summarizing the visit. AI-generated information can contain errors or omissions.

My clinician remains responsible for reviewing, editing as appropriate, and approving the final clinical note. The signed clinician-authored or clinician-approved note is the permanent clinical documentation produced through this workflow.

6. Temporary Retention

Thymos generally retains temporary transcripts and raw audio for 90 days; and AI-generated draft summaries for 90 days.

These materials are then automatically deleted unless a valid legal hold or other applicable legal requirement requires retention.

7. Access

Access to temporary recordings, transcripts, and draft summaries is restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate role-based need, including selected clinical leadership; quality personnel; administrators; and engineers.

8. Use After the Final Note Is Signed

After my clinician signs the final clinical note, Thymos will not use the temporary audio, transcript, or AI-generated summary to make or change decisions about my diagnosis; treatment plan; medications; eligibility for care; or other individual clinical management.

9. Third-Party Processing

Thymos may use healthcare technology providers acting under appropriate confidentiality, privacy, security, and Business Associate obligations to process encounter content. Clinical content processed by such providers may itself contain identifiable Protected Health Information.

Thymos does not permit identifiable patient recordings or PHI to be used by third-party providers to train general-purpose AI models for the third party's independent purposes.

10. Internal Algorithm Improvement

Identifiable recordings may be used internally for Thymos quality assurance and validation consistent with applicable law.

For algorithm development beyond ordinary healthcare operations, Thymos seeks to use HIPAA-de-identified information whenever reasonably feasible.

11. Withdrawal or Declining Recording

I may decline recording before an encounter is recorded. Declining recording does not prevent me from receiving the telehealth visit.

Withdrawal of consent does not undo processing or quality activities lawfully performed before withdrawal.

12. Consent

By separately accepting this Audio Recording and AI-Assisted Documentation Consent in the Thymos application, and by affirmatively agreeing again before an individual recorded visit, I consent to the recording and processing described above.

Addendum D — California Medical Services Arbitration Agreement

Article 1 — Arbitration of Professional Negligence

"It is understood that any dispute as to medical malpractice, that is as to whether any medical services rendered under this contract were unnecessary or unauthorized or were improperly, negligently or incompetently rendered, will be determined by submission to arbitration as provided by California law, and not by a lawsuit or resort to court process except as California law provides for judicial review of arbitration proceedings. Both parties to this contract, by entering into it, are giving up their constitutional right to have any such dispute decided in a court of law before a jury, and instead are accepting the use of arbitration."

This Article concerns disputes alleging professional negligence or medical malpractice in connection with medical services provided by Thymos Provider Group, PC or healthcare professionals acting through it.

Article 2 — Agreement to Arbitrate

Subject to applicable California law, the patient and Thymos Provider Group, PC agree that disputes concerning professional negligence or medical malpractice covered by this Agreement will be resolved through binding arbitration rather than a jury or court trial, except to the extent California law provides otherwise.

Article 3 — Arbitration Administration

Unless applicable law requires otherwise, arbitration will be administered by JAMS before a neutral arbitrator experienced in healthcare disputes.

Proceedings will be conducted in accordance with applicable California law and any arbitration rules that do not conflict with mandatory requirements governing medical-services arbitration agreements.

Article 4 — Claims Covered

This Agreement applies to claims within the scope permitted by California law concerning alleged professional negligence in the rendering of medical services by:

  • •Thymos Provider Group, PC;
  • •physicians or other healthcare professionals providing care through Provider Group; and
  • •other persons or entities legally entitled to invoke the Agreement with respect to such medical services.

The scope of this Agreement will not be construed more broadly than applicable law permits.

Article 5 — Claims Not Waivable

Nothing in this Agreement waives:

  • •rights or remedies that California law does not permit the parties to waive;
  • •applicable rights to judicial review of arbitration;
  • •legally nonwaivable public injunctive relief; or
  • •any other right that cannot legally be subjected to arbitration.

Article 6 — Relationship to General Terms

The general arbitration provision in the Thymos Terms of Service governs non-clinical contractual and platform disputes within its scope. This Medical Services Arbitration Agreement governs professional-negligence and medical-malpractice disputes within its scope.

If the two provisions conflict concerning a professional-negligence claim, this separately accepted Medical Services Arbitration Agreement and applicable California law control.

Article 7 — Thirty-Day Rescission Right

After signing this Agreement, the patient may rescind the Medical Services Arbitration Agreement by written notice within 30 days of signature, as provided by California law.

A rescission notice may be sent to legal@thymoshealth.com or mailed to:

Thymos Provider Group, PC
Attn: Legal
679 S. Eliseo Dr.
Greenbrae, CA 94904

Thymos will preserve the date and method of any timely rescission request.

Article 8 — Electronic Agreement

Where legally permitted, this Agreement may be presented and signed electronically. Thymos will maintain an electronic record showing:

  • •the version presented;
  • •the date and time presented;
  • •the identity/account associated with the acceptance;
  • •the date and time of signature or affirmative acceptance; and
  • •any subsequent timely rescission.

Required California Notice Before Signature

NOTICE: BY SIGNING THIS CONTRACT YOU ARE AGREEING TO HAVE ANY ISSUE OF MEDICAL MALPRACTICE DECIDED BY NEUTRAL ARBITRATION AND YOU ARE GIVING UP YOUR RIGHT TO A JURY OR COURT TRIAL. SEE ARTICLE 1 OF THIS CONTRACT.

This Medical Services Arbitration Agreement is tacitly accepted by agreeing to the general Thymos Terms of Service.

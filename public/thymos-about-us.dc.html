From the Founder

Meet Founder, Payal Bhandari, M.D.

After twenty-five years of practicing primary care and integrative functional medicine, Dr. Bhandari saw the same pattern repeat itself: patients with real, debilitating symptoms circling the healthcare system for years — misdiagnosed, dismissed, or handed a prescription that treated the flare instead of the disease.

She launched San Francisco's first accountable care organization and its largest integrative functional center. She was president of the Academy of Integrative Health and Medicine's San Francisco chapter since 2015, and chief medical advisor to multiple health technology startups. As an autoimmune patient herself, and mother of two daughters diagnosed with autoimmune disorders, she has refused to accept the status quo.

"Patients need the right quarterback to care for their whole being — mind, body, and spirit — and treat their ailments early enough to reverse it."

Thymos Health was born out of that conviction. It pairs expert clinicians with advanced diagnostics across 150+ autoimmune conditions, so patients can spend less time searching for answers and more time getting better.

Our Mission

The Healthcare We All Deserve

We understand your struggles of not feeling heard by a healthcare provider or getting the right diagnosis and treatment in a timely manner.

We are building the kind of healthcare we all deserve – compassionate, courageous, and inspiring. We drive data-driven diagnoses and personalized care that address diseases at the root cause.

Ongoing tracking of your symptoms, wearable data, and diagnostic tests allows us to continuously optimize your care and prevent any terrible health outcomes.

We are always by your side, helping you feel like your best self — physically, mentally, and emotionally.

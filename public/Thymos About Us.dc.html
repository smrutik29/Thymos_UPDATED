thymos. θυμός.

Thymos is ancient Greek for courage, vitality, and spirit. It is the middle realm of consciousness – sitting between reason (logos) and desire (thelo). It is the driving emotions of the human soul.

Thymos is associated with our internal physiological processes of thought, emotion, volition, and motivation, ensuring the unity of mind, body and spirit.

Our Story

Thymos was founded by integrative functional medicine physician Dr. Payal Bhandari, MD. As a patient herself and mother of two daughters who have suffered with autoimmune disorders, she is not willing to accept anything less than solving these complex, mysterious issues at the root level.

She launched the first nationally-recognized accountable care organization in San Francisco and built the largest private integrative functional medical center. Determined to find anything that could reverse her children's condition, Dr. Bhandari went on a 15 year journey to understand the root cause of autoimmune disorders.

She combines the best in scientifically based Western and Eastern Medicine to understand the root cause and tailor care based on each patient's unique physical, emotional, psychological, and epigenetic profile. Dr. Bhandari has successfully treated more than 5,000 patients with various autoimmune disorders and complex, mysterious conditions.

We understand your struggles of not feeling heard by a healthcare provider or getting the right diagnosis and treatment in a timely manner.

We are building the kind of healthcare we all deserve – compassionate, courageous, and inspiring. We drive data-driven diagnoses and personalized care that address diseases at the root cause.

Ongoing tracking of your symptoms, wearable data, and diagnostic tests allows us to continuously optimize your care and prevent any terrible health outcomes.

We are always by your side, helping you feel like your best self — physically, mentally, and emotionally.

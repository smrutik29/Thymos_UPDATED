If you recognize your condition or medication above, Thymos provides dedicated clinical support to explore questions your doctor may not have time to address.

What the program includes
  • A licensed Thymos clinician who learns your full history and works alongside your existing care team
  • Personalized support for exploring a medically supervised medication taper — only if appropriate for you
  • Ongoing check-ins, symptom tracking, and clinical guidance throughout your journey
  • No change to your current prescriptions, coverage, or relationship with your doctor
  • No cost to you. Covered entirely by your benefits program.

Participation is completely voluntary. All clinical interactions with Thymos are confidential and have no impact on your employment, your existing coverage, or your access to your current medications.

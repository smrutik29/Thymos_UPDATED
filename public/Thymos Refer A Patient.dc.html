Refer a patient

Use this form to refer a patient to Thymos Health if they have been diagnosed with autoimmune disorder(s) or if autoimmunity is suspected.

After receiving your referral, our team verifies eligibility and contacts the patient directly to complete enrollment and better understand their needs.

We'll notify your team once they're enrolled or if we have any questions. For questions or referral updates, contact us at referrals@thymoshealth.com.
